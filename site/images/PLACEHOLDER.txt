The 12 images are intentionally omitted from the submission archive, per the
assignment instruction not to include them.

To run this site with images, place the 12 provided files here as:

    sticker_1.avif  ...  sticker_12.avif

and generate the PNG fallbacks used by browsers without AVIF support:

    for i in $(seq 1 12); do
      sips -s format png "sticker_$i.avif" --out "sticker_$i.png"   # macOS
    done

The filenames and captions are defined in site/data/images.json.
